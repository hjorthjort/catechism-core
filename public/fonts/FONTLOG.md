# Conciliar Text

Conciliar Text is a screen-reading type family made for CCC Explorer. Its broad old-style proportions, restrained contrast, sturdy serifs, and editorial italics are inspired by the calm typography of the printed *Catechism of the Catholic Church*, without reproducing that book's typeface.

Version 1.000 was built from the OFL-licensed Source Serif 4 outlines at an 18-point optical size. The derivative changes include:

- a distinct family name and font metadata;
- a slightly wider drawing for more open counters on screens;
- expanded advance widths for a less crowded reading rhythm;
- custom regular and semibold weight instances;
- static WOFF2 output for predictable browser rendering.

The four files cover regular, italic, semibold, and semibold italic styles. Conciliar Text remains licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `OFL.txt` in this directory.
